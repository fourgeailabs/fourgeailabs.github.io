About the forge

FourgeAI LABS is the studio name of Eric Collins, a telecommunications professional and independent developer in Surprise, Arizona, building under the DevForge Pro banner. The rules here are simple: no ads, no data harvesting, no dark patterns — software that does its job and respects the person using it. And for anything sold here: buy it on one platform, you own it on all of them — Android, macOS and Windows, one purchase, never twice for the same title. When he isn't shipping apps, he's writing the science-fiction manuscript The Vanguard Paradox — the world Desertfall calls home.
